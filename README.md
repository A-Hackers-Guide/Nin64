# retroboot

A fancy in-browser boot picker that lets you walk into stylised recreations of classic console home menus — **Wii, Wii U, Switch, GameCube, N64, NES, SNES** — and, when your machine is powerful enough, play your own ROMs inside them.

Everything runs locally in your browser. No uploads, no server, no accounts. Hardware checks are done client-side and the emulator cores run in WebAssembly in your tab.

## Legal

This project is a fan tribute. All menu recreations are hand-built HTML/CSS/JS and ship **no** Nintendo code, art, firmware, BIOS, or ROMs. You supply your own legally-owned ROM files at play time and they stay in your browser's memory — they are never uploaded anywhere.

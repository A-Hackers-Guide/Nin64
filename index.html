About retroboot

A fan-built, local-only portal into stylised recreations of classic console home menus.

How it works

Your browser is benchmarked on load — CPU cores, RAM, GPU renderer, and WebAssembly feature support feed a composite score. Each console has a tier; a card only unlocks when your machine clears it. When a console supports in-browser emulation it loads EmulatorJS (MIT) against a ROM you add from your local library.

What's here & what isn't

  • Here: hand-built HTML/CSS menu recreations, synthesized UI sounds, hardware gating, local ROM storage, per-console settings with "Export as standalone HTML".
  • Not here: no Nintendo code, art, firmware, or ROMs ship with this repo. Supply your own legally-owned files.
  • Can't emulate yet: Wii U and Switch — no practical browser cores. Their menus still run; games fall through to a "use a desktop emulator" message.
